The Blog and Whatever else

This is the Blog Section. I want to turn this next section into a way to display my artwork but also my thoughts. A true blog seems to be an online journal. A living diary with doodles, thoughts, maybe links to more creative places or where people find inspiration. That is what I want to turn this space into. A place for people to look inside my mind.

This portrait I created years ago Is a living idea of a character that I use in several works of my art. She is seen in most works of mine as a representation for myself, or maybe a more idealistic version of myself.
