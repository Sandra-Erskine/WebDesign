Project Hot Pink

This series explores the power of the color Hot Pink. As a child I loved pink, then I turned away from it as an angsty teen, preferring turquoise. As an adult, I came to understand that I still loved pink, and how powerful of a color it can be. This is examined in the juxtaposition of the two colors, Pink and Teal. They both have their place, no greater than the other. In putting them together, their combination is more powerful than either color alone.

Project Demon and Alien

This series explores the relationship between comics and people. How many different types of characters can we identify with and why? These creatures I came up with / got inspiration from other sources are representations of emotions we feel every day as humans. By showcasing them as non-human, I can show humans our own ego and how attached it is to the body and ourselves.
