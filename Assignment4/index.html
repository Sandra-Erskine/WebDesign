Future Gallery Ideas

This website will act as a guinea pig website for my future portfolio. I want to make the design eye-catching, and less reflective of the HGTV websites that already exist. Let's get experimental!

Some ideas I might include are a portfolio section, images, or interactive features. This project will help me practice my web development skills and creativity.

Project Hot Pink

This series explores the power of the color Hot Pink. As a child I loved pink, then I turned away from it as an angsty teen, preferring turquoise. As an adult, I realized I still loved pink and its power. Juxtaposing Pink and Teal shows how they complement each other.

Project Demon and Alien

This series explores the relationship between comics and people. The creatures are representations of everyday human emotions. Showing them as non-human highlights our ego and attachment to the body.
